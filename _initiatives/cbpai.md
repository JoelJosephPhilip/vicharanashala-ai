<p class="idt-lead">CBPAI — the Capacity Building Program on Artificial Intelligence, funded by the Ministry of Education, Government of India (MoE / UGC-MMTTP). With so many AI programmes running nationwide, why choose this one? Because we pivot away from a "tools-first" approach: AI literacy is not merely about mastering prompts or new software — it is about learning how to think alongside a new kind of intelligence.</p>

## Who this is for

This programme is designed for faculty across all disciplines — STEM, humanities, social sciences, commerce, law, and health sciences. Every cohort begins from first principles, assuming no prior technical knowledge. The goal is that a faculty member from law and one from computer science can sit in the same cohort, and neither feels left behind or unchallenged. Delivery is fully online and asynchronous-first, so faculty from universities across all states and UTs — particularly from Tier 2 and Tier 3 institutions underrepresented in AI training initiatives — can access quality capacity building without disrupting their teaching schedules.

## A benchmarking instrument for Indian higher education

A core research output of CBPAI is a 26-item AI-literacy benchmarking instrument, currently in expert validation and pilot testing. It is designed to measure faculty AI readiness across disciplines — not just STEM — with a pedagogy-first lens.

<p class="idt-note">There is no comparable standardised instrument currently available in the Indian higher-education context — making this an original contribution to the field.</p>
