<p class="idt-lead">Summership is a structured, eight-week virtual internship run by the Vicharanashala Lab, built around a difficult question: can every student — regardless of which institution they attend — get access to a high-quality research internship at an IIT?</p>

<p class="idt-note">The answer, since 2026, is yes.</p>

## How it works

<div class="idt-cards">
  <div class="idt-card">
    <div class="idt-card-icon"><i class="ph ph-robot"></i></div>
    <div class="idt-card-name">Run autonomously</div>
    <p class="idt-card-desc">Registration, application screening, AI-conducted interviews, offer-letter issuance, and ongoing engagement all run end-to-end on Samagama and Yaksha — letting a 4-person core team run what would otherwise need 20–30 coordinators, without sacrificing quality or structure.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="idt-card">
    <div class="idt-card-icon"><i class="ph ph-users-three"></i></div>
    <div class="idt-card-name">Inclusive by design</div>
    <p class="idt-card-desc">Tier 2 and Tier 3 institution students — routinely locked out of research exposure by network gatekeeping — are equally eligible. A standardised, AI-conducted screening removes the informal filters that typically disadvantage first-generation and non-metro students.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="idt-card">
    <div class="idt-card-icon"><i class="ph ph-buildings"></i></div>
    <div class="idt-card-name">Backed by IIT Ropar</div>
    <p class="idt-card-desc">Affiliated with IIT Ropar and aligned with the lab's broader mandate under the NEP 2020 push for structured student development and research exposure.</p>
  </div>
</div>
