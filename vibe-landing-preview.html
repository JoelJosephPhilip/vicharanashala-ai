Why Choose ViBe?

ViBe is an online learning platform that helps students stay engaged through smart quizzes. AI-powered monitoring supports the learner to stay focused without feeling invasive.

  • Micro-Learning — Long lectures automatically split into short, focused parts.
  • Smart Assessments — Quizzes appear during the video, not after it.
  • Active Verification — AI checks focus signals in real time, reviewed by a person if flagged.
